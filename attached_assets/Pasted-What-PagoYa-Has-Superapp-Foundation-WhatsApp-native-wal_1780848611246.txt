What PagoYa Has (Superapp Foundation)

	•	WhatsApp-native wallet + bill pay (SIPREL/Taecel)
	•	Loyalty tiers (Bronce/Plata/Oro) — proto-behavioral credit scoring
	•	Street team colonia distribution
	•	Gift cards / PIN vouchers (Reloadly)
	•	SPEI inbound + Conekta card top-up
	•	AI agent Paula for conversational UX

This is a solid payments utility layer. The gap is everything that makes people live inside an app beyond bill pay.

The Superapp Gap Analysis

1. 🎯 Daily Engagement Anchor (Biggest Gap)

Bill pay is monthly. Superapps need daily or weekly touchpoints.

What Baz has that PagoYa doesn’t:

	•	Chat / social messaging inside the app
	•	Music streaming (Baz integrates Azteca music)
	•	News feed (Azteca Digital content)
	•	TV/video content (Azteca UNO streaming)

For PagoYa, the WhatsApp Channel colonia social feed concept you already designed is the right move — it creates daily reasons to open/engage without building a media empire. This is the single highest-leverage missing piece.

2. 💰 Outbound P2P Transfers (Currently Blocked)

Users can receive SPEI inbound but can’t send money peer-to-peer yet (pending STP contract). P2P is the stickiest financial feature in any wallet — it’s what made CashApp, Venmo, and M-Pesa viral. Every time a user sends money to a friend, that friend becomes a potential user.

Priority: High. This is table stakes for a wallet superapp.

3. 🛍️ Marketplace / Local Commerce Layer

Baz lets micro-businesses create online stores and accept QR payments. PagoYa has the street team colonia presence to do something Baz can’t — a hyperlocal merchant directory and QR acceptance network for PV tiendas, taquerías, and chanclas vendors.

Merchants become nodes. Customers pay via PagoYa. Both sides are locked in.

4. 💳 Credit / Lending Signal (Medium-Term)

Your loyalty tier system is already capturing behavioral data. The next step is a micro-credit or BNPL product — even something small like “Adelanto PagoYa” (advance your next bill payment). Baz offers loans up to $70,000 MXN. This is what converts a utility into a financial relationship.

The data you’re already collecting (payment frequency, on-time behavior, tier progression) is the underwriting model. You just need the product wrapper.

5. 📱 Savings / Yield Product

Mercado Pago and PicPay both offer in-app savings with yield. Even 4–6% on idle wallet balances keeps money in the PagoYa wallet instead of users topping up only when they need to pay a bill. This dramatically increases average wallet balance and float.

6. 🤝 QR Merchant Acceptance Network

Right now PagoYa is consumer-side only. Adding a merchant-facing QR payment acceptance tool — even just a static QR linked to a merchant wallet — creates a closed-loop economy in each colonia. Money paid to merchants stays in PagoYa. This is what Baz and M-Pesa both built to create ecosystem lock-in.

7. 🎮 Gamification Beyond Tiers

The loyalty tiers are a start, but superapps use:

	•	Streaks (pay 3 bills in a row, earn bonus points)
	•	Referral leaderboards by colonia
	•	Seasonal challenges (summer bill-pay sprint)
	•	Cashback surprises at milestone transactions

This keeps Oro-tier users engaged and gives Bronce users a visible path upward.
