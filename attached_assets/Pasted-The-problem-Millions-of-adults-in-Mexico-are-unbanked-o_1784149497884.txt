The problem
Millions of adults in Mexico are unbanked or thinly-banked, which locks them out of formal credit even when they're reliably paying bills every month. There's no good way to translate "pays CFE and rent on time" into a credit signal a lender can use.
The product
PagoYa is a WhatsApp-native bill payment platform — people pay things like CFE (electricity) directly through WhatsApp, no app download required. The flagship CTA on the site is "Paga tu CFE ahora." Fee structure is a flat 25 MXN per transaction, and new users get a 150 MXN signup bonus credited immediately on OTP verification (no OXXO cash-load required to unlock it).
Underneath the payment layer sits the real engine: PTI (Predictive Trust Index), a behavioral credit-scoring system that turns payment consistency, diversity of billers, and other behavioral signals into a trust/creditworthiness score. PTI is being built as a standalone, licensable product — it's now at v5.0-rc1, and importantly it's designed to sit outside Banxico's SIC (credit bureau) authorization requirement by operating as a "predictive indicator of payment capacity" rather than a credit history/report — a deliberate regulatory-moat play.
On top of PTI is Paula, a WhatsApp-based "Trust Trajectory Counselor" — she runs users through a financial literacy curriculum (Modules 1–5, PTI-gated), tells them what's missing to qualify for credit (NOT_YET → APPROACHING → READY), and eventually facilitates consent-based handoff to a lending partner once someone hits the readiness bar (PTI ≥ 80, 90-day streak, ≥3 billers, KYC complete, fraud-free, literacy ≥ 3).

Traction, honestly stated
Soft launch has been modest so far — 11 real users between May 8 and June 30, only 3 wallet transactions total, none past the wallet-load stage. This reads as early data-maturity rather than a product problem, but it's worth being upfront about rather than glossing over if this profile is investor-facing. Separately, there've been 3 organic signups from Monterrey via SEO landing pages at zero CAC — a small but real signal.
