PagoYa is a WhatsApp-native digital wallet and behavioral credit engine for Mexico's 65 million unbanked adults. No app download, no bank account, no credit card required.

At the core is Paula, an AI agent powered by Claude (Anthropic) that handles the full payment experience conversationally. A user texts Paula the name of their service — CFE, Telmex, Telcel, IZZI, or any of 20+ billers — Paula auto-fills details, confirms the amount, and processes payment in under two minutes. Official receipts with Banco de México transaction IDs arrive via WhatsApp instantly. Flat fee: $25 MXN per transaction, no hidden charges.

Users fund their wallet three ways: cash at any OXXO via a barcode voucher, SPEI transfer using a unique CLABE per user, or debit card via Conekta. Every transaction flows through SIPREL and STP — Mexico's national payment infrastructure.

Beyond bill payment, PagoYa offers digital gift cards across three verticals: entertainment (Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, Max, Cinépolis, Google Play), convenience (Uber, Uber Eats, Amazon), and retail (Liverpool, Soriana, Starbucks). PINs delivered via WhatsApp within minutes.

Every action feeds the PagoYa Trust Index (PTI) — a proprietary behavioral credit score across seven dimensions: payment streak, biller diversity, wallet balance, load-to-spend ratio, KYC verification, mission completions, and account age. The PTI is the first formal financial record most users have ever had. In Phase 2 it underwrites micro-credit at PTI scores above 60. In Phase 3 it becomes a licensable API for lenders, insurers, and US diaspora banks.

Retention is built on a full engagement stack: loyalty tiers (Bronce, Plata, Oro), eight behavioral missions with WhatsApp notifications on completion, weekly frequency bonuses at the 3rd, 5th, and 10th payment, a scratch card, a spin-to-win wheel at registration, and a monthly $2,000 MXN Gran Premio draw. Every mechanic reinforces the behaviors that build PTI score.

Distribution runs on a street team rep model. Reps cover defined colonias, earn $150 MXN per signup and $500 MXN per first completed payment, and track commissions via a live dashboard. The model saturates one colonia before expanding — launching in Puerto Vallarta's 5 de Diciembre, Versalles, and Emiliano Zapata before scaling to Guadalajara and Mexico City.

PagoYa is live at pagoyamx.com — a PWA on Google Cloud, processing real transactions, with STP/SPEI outbound contract in final stages.
