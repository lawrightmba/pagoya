PagoYa is a live, deployed fintech platform at pagoyamx.com. Mexican consumers use it today to pay utilities, telecom, and streaming bills in under two minutes — without a bank account. But the more complete description is this: PagoYa is a WhatsApp-native financial services platform powered by an agentic AI called Paula. For the 54 million underbanked adults in Mexico, WhatsApp is not a messaging app — it is their operating system. Paula meets them there.

PagoYa is the primary product and the website for YC consideration. It encompasses both a core bill payment and wallet platform and a set of purpose-built verticals — including PagoSeguro, our rent collection vertical, which lives at pagoseguromx.com. PagoSeguro is not a separate company — it is PagoYa deployed specifically for landlords, tenants, and property managers, running on the same underlying infrastructure.

What Is Working and Shipped Today?
Agentic AI — Paula (the centerpiece of the product)
Paula is a Claude-powered AI agent deployed across two channels simultaneously: WhatsApp (inbound) and an in-app floating chat widget on every page of the product. She is not a FAQ bot. Paula runs a tool-use loop with access to live user data and takes real actions on the user's behalf.
Her tools today:

get_wallet_balance — tells the user their exact MXN balance in real time
get_payment_history — retrieves and narrates recent transactions
get_pending_oxxo — checks whether a cash deposit is still pending confirmation
escalate_to_support — hands off to a human agent via WhatsApp with full context
prepare_bill_payment — validates a payment intent (service, reference, amount), checks wallet balance, stages a payment for 2FA confirmation, returns a fee-inclusive summary for the user to confirm or cancel

On WhatsApp, any inbound message routes through the Paula agent loop. First-contact messages are screened for rep referral codes before entering the agent flow. Paula replies in natural Spanish, handles multi-turn conversations, and escalates gracefully when a question is out of scope. In-app, the chat widget persists across every screen with local storage session memory, a typing indicator, and an escalation banner. After every successful payment, the app automatically opens Paula's chat after 4 seconds with a pre-filled context message — turning a transactional receipt moment into a retention touch.

Paula Payment Initiation from WhatsApp — The Key Differentiator
Users initiate and confirm bill payments entirely within WhatsApp — no app download, no browser, no bank account required. Paula handles service lookup, balance validation, fee disclosure, and payment execution in a single conversation thread.

MercadoPago, Spin, and Cashi all require an app open for payment confirmation. Paula doesn't. The entire transaction — from intent to folio — happens inside a WhatsApp thread. This is not a UX preference; for the 54 million underbanked Mexicans whose primary device has limited storage and intermittent data, avoiding an app download is the difference between a sale and a lost user.

The flow:

User texts "paga mi CFE" (or Telmex, Izzi, Totalplay, any of 20+ billers)
Paula performs service lookup, validates the reference, and checks wallet balance
Paula replies: "💳 CFE · Ref: XXXXXXXXXXXX · $350 MXN + $25 comisión = $375 total. Responde SÍ para confirmar o NO para cancelar."
User replies "sí" — Paula executes the payment from wallet and returns the folio
User replies "no" — payment cancelled, session cleared
Pending payments expire automatically after 5 minutes with no response

The 2FA confirmation is handled deterministically at the session layer — not by the language model — so "sí" to a pending payment is always a payment execution, regardless of conversation context. Paula cannot be confused or tricked into executing a payment by an ambiguous message. Pending payment state is fully DB-persisted with SQL-enforced expiry — no payment can execute against a stale or in-memory pending state.

AI Bill Autofill
The home screen is a natural-language input. Claude parses "CFE 350 pesos" and pre-fills the service, amount, and reference number in one shot. Users who have never used a fintech app understand it immediately — because it works like texting.

Core Payment Infrastructure
Bill payment integrates SIPREL and Evoluciona with automatic failover across 20+ billers. Flat fee of $25 MXN per transaction. Wallet debited atomically per payment.

Digital Wallet — Three Funding Channels
OXXO cash deposits (barcode vouchers generated in-app), card top-ups (Conekta tokenization with saved-card support), and SPEI inbound bank transfers (webhook-verified). All three work today.

Loyalty & Rewards
Users earn points on every payment (1 pt / $10 MXN, Bronze / Silver / Gold tier multipliers). Points are redeemable for wallet credit or a free-transaction token that waives the $25 MXN fee — enforced atomically inside the payment DB transaction.

Street-Team Referral Network with WhatsApp OTP Onboarding
Reps recruit users in the field using unique referral links and QR codes. New users sign up via a three-screen flow: form to WhatsApp OTP to bonus wallet credit ($25–$50 MXN incentive). Rep commissions are $5 MXN per confirmed payment with a 7-day hold. The rep earnings dashboard includes a self-serve recruitment section with a shareable signup link, a downloadable QR code, and live recruitment stats pulling from live DB queries. International phone support is built in for migrant and diaspora referrals.

Conversion Funnel — Three-Touch Coral Architecture
Every landing page visitor encounters a structured three-touch acquisition funnel: a dismissible announcement bar (first element below nav, remembered in localStorage after dismissal), a hero badge at the emotional peak of the headline, and a bonus strip for deliberate evaluators. Each touch drives to the same registration CTA. The funnel was designed specifically for iPhone SE and small-screen Android viewports where a single browser URL bar can bury below-fold content — the announcement bar ensures the $25 MXN offer is the second thing every mobile visitor reads, regardless of screen size.

Post-OTP Activation Screen
After completing phone OTP verification, new users land on a dedicated activation screen before reaching the dashboard. Zone 1 confirms the welcome bonus landed (coral checkmark, live bonus amount pulled from config). Zone 2 shows the live wallet balance with a pulsing loading state — never displaying $0 during fetch. Zone 3 presents a single primary CTA ("Paga tu primera cuenta →"), four biller quick-launch chips (CFE, Telmex, Izzi, Agua) that deep-link to the payment form with service pre-filled, and a WhatsApp deep link introducing Paula. The screen is a one-time experience — a session flag prevents it from re-appearing after the user has seen it, and the browser history stack is replaced so back navigation skips it entirely.
Automated Lifecycle Nudges — Two-Touch Post-Registration Sequence
T+10 minutes — Activation nudge: Every new user who completes registration receives a personalized WhatsApp message from Paula exactly 10 minutes after sign-up. The message is skipped if they have already transacted. It references their first name, confirms the bonus amount, and includes a direct deep-link back into the payment flow.

T+48 hours — Retention nudge: Users who register but do not transact within 48 hours receive a second WhatsApp message reminding them of their available balance and offering Paula as an immediate payment assistant. The nudge fires within a 48–72 hour window via an hourly cron, respects a deduplication guard, and skips users who have already made a real payment. Both nudges are DB-backed with admin visibility in the command center.

Admin Command Center
Live at pagoyamx.com/command-center.html. Includes a 👤 Usuarios tab with five real-time stat tiles: Total usuarios, Nudge enviado (T+10 activation reach), Bono acreditado (funded wallet rate), Pantalla vista (activation screen reach), and Retención (T+48h nudge reach). A full user table shows nudge status, bonus status, welcome screen status, signup source, and rep attribution for every registered user. The command center auto-refreshes every 60 seconds.

Signup_source Tracking
Every registration writes either web_organic or rep_referral to the users table. This enables a clean split at any point between street team-driven growth and organic web conversion — the two acquisition channels can be measured and optimized independently.

PWA Push Notifications — iOS and Android
PagoYa is a fully installable Progressive Web App on both iOS and Android. Service worker at /sw.js handles push events and notification click routing. Web App Manifest enables "Add to Home Screen" installation with no App Store required. Every successful bill payment automatically triggers a push notification to the paying user's device — the same experience as a native app, with zero App Store friction.

Why This Is Different From Other LATAM Fintech?
MercadoPago, Spin, and Cashi are app-first products — every payment confirmation requires an app open. For the underbanked user with a $150 Android phone and 2GB of storage, that is a real barrier. Paula eliminates it. A user with zero apps installed, zero bank account, and zero fintech experience can pay their electricity bill in four WhatsApp messages.

Most fintech in Mexico is still trying to convince underbanked users to adopt a new interface. We deployed AI into the interface they already use twelve hours a day. Paula on WhatsApp means a user can check their balance, ask why a payment failed, get their receipt narrated back to them, or pay their CFE bill — without opening anything, without a login screen, in the same thread they use to talk to their family.

PagoSeguro applies the same logic to housing: landlords in Mexico still collect rent via cash or informal bank transfers with no paper trail. PagoSeguro gives them digital collection, automatic receipts, and WhatsApp confirmation — without asking them to change how they think about rent.

The product is not the app. The product is Paula, and the app — along with PagoSeguro — is Paula's backend
