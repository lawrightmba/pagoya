## About PagoYa

PagoYa is a mobile-first bill payment and digital wallet platform designed for underbanked and unbanked users in Mexico. It enables anyone with a smartphone to pay utility bills, mobile top-ups, and services — with or without a traditional bank account. PagoYa charges a flat platform fee of $15 MXN per transaction with no hidden costs.

PagoYa is operated by Longview Meridian Technologies and is based in Mexico.

## What PagoYa Does

- Bill payments for 26+ services including CFE (electricity), Telmex, Izzi, Totalplay, Sky, Megacable, and more
- Mobile top-ups (recargas) for Telcel, AT&T, Movistar, and other carriers
- Digital wallet with OXXO cash-in via Conekta (no bank account required)
- SPEI/STP payment rails for fast interbank transfers
- Rent payments via integrated PagoSeguro vertical
- SIPREL and Evoluciona provider adapters with automatic failover
- Auto-failover between payment providers for maximum uptime

## Who PagoYa Serves

- Underbanked and unbanked adults in Mexico
- Mobile-first users in mid-sized cities and rural areas
- Renters and landlords via the integrated PagoSeguro vertical
- Migrant workers and remittance recipients

## Key Differentiators

- No bank account required — cash-in via OXXO at 20,000+ locations nationwide
- 26-service catalog with single-app convenience
- Flat $15 MXN fee — no percentage-based charges
- Auto-failover between SIPREL and Evoluciona ensures near-100% uptime

## Pricing

Flat fee: $15 MXN per transaction. No percentage-based charges. No monthly subscription required for basic users.
