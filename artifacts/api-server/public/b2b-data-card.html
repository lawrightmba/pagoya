1. What the PTI Measures

The PagoYa Trust Index is a behavioral creditworthiness score derived entirely from a user's activity inside the PagoYa digital wallet — bill payments, session patterns, wallet transactions, WhatsApp interactions, and peer-to-peer transfers. It is designed for populations with no formal credit bureau file (thin-file or no-file), where transactional and behavioral data is the primary signal available.

The PTI does not use: bureau data, bank account data, employment records, income declarations, or demographic surveys. All inputs are observed, not self-reported.

v4.0 additions: 7 new behavioral signals integrated into scoring — advance payment days (proactive vs. reactive payer), self-initiated ratio (unprompted payments), routine score (login hour + DOM variance composite), financial curiosity index (proactive Paula topics / total), payment amount volatility (obligation consistency), device consistency score (identity stability), and recovery score (return rate after payment gaps). Paula WhatsApp messages now classified into 7 topic buckets powering the curiosity index.
Regulatory basis: Data collection and scoring are performed under the user's explicit consent captured at registration, in compliance with Mexico's Ley para Regular las Instituciones de Tecnología Financiera (Ley Fintech, 2018) and the Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares (LFPDPPP).

2. Score Architecture — 4 Dimensions (PTI 0–100)

PR
Payment Reliability
max 30 pts
  • Payment streak — consecutive months with ≥1 confirmed payment (1pt/month, cap 13)
  • Payment day consistency — low DOM std-dev across 6 months (max 4)
  • Advance payment days v4.0 — avg days paid before due date; ≥7 days early = max 8pts
  • Self-initiated ratio v4.0 — share of payments opened without a Paula reminder (max 5)
BC
Behavioral Consistency
max 20 pts
  • Session cadence — login days per 30-day window (max 3)
  • Routine score v4.0 — composite: login-hour std-dev + payment DOM std-dev; low variance = structured habits (max 3)
  • Game & mission engagement — loyalty feature activity proxy (max 3)
  • Wallet load rhythm — load frequency std-dev over 90 days (max 2)
  • Paula interaction depth — WhatsApp AI queries + 2FA completion rate (max 3)
  • Push notification engagement — open rate, last 30 days (max 2)
  • Financial curiosity index v4.0 — proactive topic ratio (savings/PTI inquiries ÷ total Paula messages, 90d) (max 4)
ED
Engagement Depth
max 25 pts
  • Biller diversity — distinct service providers paid (max 6)
  • KYC verified — identity verification tier: simplified (7) or full (10)
  • Spend category mix — utility bill ratio + high-value intent clicks (max 4)
  • Signup utilization speed — hours from registration to first payment (max 2)
  • Device consistency v4.0 — days on current device; same device ≥120 days = max 3pts; identity stability signal
CF
Cash-Flow Stability
max 25 pts
  • Wallet balance — current balance; maintaining positive float (max 9)
  • Load/spend ratio — total loads vs. total spend, 90-day window (max 5)
  • Payment amount volatility v4.0 — coefficient of variation across biller amounts; low CV = stable obligations (max 4)
  • P2P network activity — wallet transfer send count + recipient diversity (max 3)
  • Account age — days since registration (max 4)

Payday Detection
Load Timing Pattern
dominant_payday_window — day-of-month cluster: 1-7, 8-15, 16-20, 21-31

Bill Obligation Profile
monthly_bill_obligations — avg monthly bill spend (MXN, 90d)
wallet_load_to_bill_ratio — loads ÷ obligations; >1.0 = surplus capacity
essential_bill_ratio — share of bills that are CFE / agua / gas / predial
Payment Quality
Per-Transaction Scoring
payment_quality_score — 0–10 per transaction: +2 self-initiated, +2 full amount, +1 SPEI channel
bill_priority_tier — essential / connectivity / discretionary
payment_channel — wallet_credit / oxxo / spei
Tenure Signals
Platform Engagement History
platform_tenure_days — days since registration
active_months — distinct calendar months with ≥1 confirmed payment
longest_gap_days — longest consecutive gap between payments
Trend Layer
Score Velocity & Trajectory
pti_trajectory — rising / stable / falling (based on 30d delta ≥±5pts)

v4.0 Behavioral Signals — 7 new columns on the per-user export, powered by nightly batch computation.
PR Signal
Advance Payment Days
advance_payment_days_avg — avg days before due date across all confirmed payments (90d window). Positive = proactive payer; 0 = day-of; negative = late payer.
PR Signal
Self-Initiated Ratio
self_initiated_ratio — 0.0–1.0 ratio of payments opened by the user themselves vs. triggered by Paula WhatsApp reminders. High ratio = strong intrinsic financial motivation.
BC Signal
Financial Curiosity Index
financial_curiosity_index — 0.0–1.0 ratio of proactive Paula topics (savings goals, PTI score inquiries) to total messages, 90-day window. Signals forward-looking financial orientation.
CF Signal
Payment Amount Volatility
payment_amount_volatility — avg coefficient of variation (CV = σ/μ) across billers over 90 days. Low CV (≤0.10) signals stable, predictable payment obligations.
ED Signal
Device Consistency Score
device_consistency_score — 0–100 score based on days since last device registration. Same device for 120+ days = score 100. Identity stability and anti-fraud proxy.
PR Signal
Recovery Score
recovery_score — 0–100 rate at which the user returned and paid within 60 days after any gap of 30+ days. Users with no gaps score 100 by default.

6. Anonymization & Privacy Model

Per-User Export (pti_export_safe)

  • The phone number (telefono) is never returned in any API response
  • Each user is identified only by a hashed_user_id = SHA-256(telefono + proprietary salt), a deterministic 64-character hex string
  • Dimension scores (PR, BC, ED, CF), B2B score, trajectory, and all granular v3.0 signals are included
  • Partners supply their own hashed_user_id mapping, obtained via a separate consent-gated matching process

Cohort Export (pti_cohort_safe)

  • k-anonymity floor: n ≥ 5. Any geographic × score band × time cohort with fewer than 5 users is fully suppressed — no partial statistics are returned
  • Geographic granularity is limited to colonia (neighborhood level); no street address or precise coordinates
  • Scores are bucketed in 10-point bands in cohort exports
  • Useful for: portfolio risk modeling, underwriting calibration, geographic credit gap analysis
Plain language: k-anonymity means any data point you receive represents at least 5 real users. It is impossible to reverse-engineer an individual's data from a cohort response.

8. Model Versioning

Every score record carries a model_version field (current: v4.0-behavioral). When the scoring formula changes materially — new signals added, weights adjusted, dimension structure changed — the version string is incremented and a migration note is published. Partners should filter by model_version when comparing scores across time periods.

9. Data Freshness

  • PTI score refresh: Monthly batch on the 1st of each month at 3:00 AM Mexico City time
  • v3.0 + v4.0 granular signals: Nightly batch at 2:00 AM Mexico City time — income regularity, payday window, obligation ratio, trend vectors, B2B score, plus all 7 v4.0 behavioral signals (advance days, self-initiated ratio, routine score, curiosity index, amount volatility, device consistency, recovery score)
  • Payment metadata: Captured at transaction time (payment_quality_score, bill_priority_tier, payment_channel stamped immediately post-confirmation)
  • API latency: Reads from pre-computed columns — typical response time <100ms
  • Historical depth: PR/ED signals use all-time history; BC/CF use 30–90 day windows; trend vectors require ≥2 nightly snapshots

10. Partner Onboarding

To receive API access, a partner must provide:

  1. Organization name and legal entity
  2. Purpose code — one of: credit_scoring, risk_assessment, portfolio_monitoring, research
  3. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — executed, covering LFPDPPP obligations and prohibiting re-identification attempts
  4. Use case description — how the score will be used in the underwriting or risk decision
  5. hashed_user_id matching methodology — how the partner will link their own user records to PagoYa hashed IDs (requires a separate bilateral consent and matching process)

Upon approval, an API key is provisioned programmatically. The raw key is delivered once via secure channel and never stored by PagoYa — only its SHA-256 hash is retained.

To request access: Contact datos@pagoyamx.com with subject line "B2B Data Access Request" and include items 1–4 above.
