Print Usage Rules

Primary print CTA: Use Gold (#F5C842) button on Forest green (#004F2D) background — this is the hero combination proven on the website. High contrast, distinctive, on-brand.
Dominant color: Forest Green (#004F2D) is the brand anchor. It should occupy the most visual space on any print piece. Never use the dark navy (#0A2540) as the primary background in print — it reads cold and tech; the green reads warm and trustworthy.
Headlines: Barlow Condensed 900 for all display text — posters, flyers, banners. Pair White (#FFFFFF) headline with Mint Light (#6EF5B0) accent line on dark backgrounds. On white, use Forest (#004F2D) as the headline color.
Orange (#FF5C1A) is reserved for urgency and OXXO payment references. Use sparingly — one callout per piece maximum. Never use orange as a background.
Amber (#F59E0B) for bonus and reward messaging only (e.g. "$150 MXN de bienvenida"). Don't mix Amber and Orange on the same piece.
Body copy print: Use Inter Regular (or Helvetica Neue Regular as substitute). Never use the screen gray tones (#94A3B8, #CBD5E1) for body copy on white print — they won't have enough contrast. Use #1F1F1F or #374151 instead.
Never use: Alert Red (#E21A0A), Blue (#3B82F6), or Indigo (#6366F1) in marketing materials — these are UI-only system colors not part of the brand identity.
CMYK printing note: The greens in this palette are rich and saturated. Request a press proof before a full run. The Forest Green (#004F2D) can shift dark on newsprint — compensate by lightening to #007A4A in those contexts.
