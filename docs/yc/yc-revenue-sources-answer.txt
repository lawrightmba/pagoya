YC APPLICATION — "Where does your revenue come from?"

---

PagoYa has three distinct revenue streams, each with a different margin profile. They run on the same infrastructure.

---

STREAM 1: BILL PAYMENT PLATFORM FEE
$25 MXN flat fee per completed transaction — charged to the user at the moment of payment.

Applies to: CFE (electricity), Telmex, Telcel, Izzi, Sky, Totalplay, and 20+ billers via SIPREL
(primary) and Evoluciona Movil (fallback). Recargas included.

This is the volume stream. The fee is intentionally lower than OXXO's variable service charges
for the same billers. We are not trying to be the highest-margin product per transaction — we
are trying to be the product users reach for every month, automatically, because it is cheaper,
faster, and they never have to leave WhatsApp.

At 100 transactions/day: $2,500 MXN/day ($150 USD)
At 1,000 transactions/day: $25,000 MXN/day ($1,500 USD)
Infrastructure supports thousands of transactions/day without additional engineering.

---

STREAM 2: DIGITAL GIFT CARD MARGIN
This is the higher-margin stream — and it launched the same week as our first paid transaction.

Revenue structure: PagoYa earns the wholesale spread on each gift card denomination sold,
plus a $25 MXN platform fee on card-funded purchases (wallet-funded purchases carry zero
platform fee — a deliberate acquisition lever for wallet engagement).

Nine brands live at launch:
- Entertainment:  Netflix ($300 / $400 / $500 / $700 MXN)
- E-commerce:     Amazon ($100 / $200 / $500 / $1,000 MXN)
- Mobile apps:    Google Play ($50 / $100 / $200 / $500 MXN)
- Rideshare:      Uber ($150 MXN)
- Food delivery:  Uber Eats ($300 MXN)
- Cinema:         Cinépolis ($60 / $120 / $210 MXN)
- Coffee:         Starbucks ($200 / $300 MXN)
- Department:     Liverpool ($500 / $1,000 / $2,000 MXN)
- Grocery:        Soriana ($500 MXN)

32 SKUs covering every major category of Mexican consumer discretionary spending.

Why this matters as a revenue stream: the gift card market in Mexico is $2.8B annually, and
virtually all of it flows through OXXO counters and supermarket kiosks — requiring a commute,
cash, and store hours. PagoYa delivers the same product in seconds via WhatsApp, 24/7, with
no cash required and no app download. The addressable market is large, the delivery mechanism
is genuinely better, and the wholesale margin on each denomination is the highest per-transaction
revenue we generate.

A single Liverpool $2,000 MXN gift card sale generates more gross revenue than 80 bill
payment transactions.

---

STREAM 3: PAGOSEGURO RENT COLLECTION FEES
Percentage-based, applied per rent payment processed through the platform.

Fee structure:
- Landlord: 1.75% of rent amount
- Tenant: 1.00% of rent amount
- Blended: ~2.75% per transaction

On a $10,000 MXN/month rent payment: $275 MXN gross revenue per transaction.
On a $20,000 MXN/month rent (common in Puerto Vallarta tourist areas): $550 MXN per
transaction — recurring, monthly, without any additional user action.

PagoSeguro is currently collecting rent for properties in Puerto Vallarta and Riviera Nayarit.
56 brokers are in an active 28-day HubSpot + Brevo email sequence. This vertical compounds
on its own — a landlord with 5 properties paying rent monthly is $1,375 MXN/month in revenue
from a single acquisition event.

NEAR-TERM REVENUE UNLOCKS (BUILT, PENDING ACTIVATION)
Each of the following adds transaction volume without adding a new fee model:

- STP / SPEI outbound: wallet-to-bank cashout unlocks the "full digital wallet" use case —
  users hold balances and transact more frequently when they can also withdraw.
- Belvo direct debit: one-tap bank-linked top-up removes the largest funding friction point
  for the 60M+ banked Mexicans we can reach.
- RENAPO KYC Level 2: higher wallet limits ($24,000 MXN/month vs. $6,000) unlock
  larger-denomination transactions — especially relevant for PagoSeguro rent payments.

None of these require engineering. They are credential activations.
